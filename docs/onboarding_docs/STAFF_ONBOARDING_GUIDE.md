## Before You Begin

### What You'll Need

To complete your profile efficiently, please have these items ready:

**Personal Information**:
- ✅ Full legal name (as it appears on your SIA license)
- ✅ Date of birth
- ✅ Current home address
- ✅ Phone number
- ✅ Email address
- ✅ National Insurance Number

**SIA License** (Required):
- ✅ Valid SIA license (not expired)
- ✅ SIA license number
- ✅ License type (Door Supervisor, Security Guard, etc.)
- ✅ Issue date and expiry date
- ✅ Clear photo or scan of your SIA license (PDF, JPG, or PNG)

**Bank Details** (For Payment):
- ✅ Bank account holder name
- ✅ Bank account number
- ✅ Sort code
- ✅ Bank name

**Emergency Contact**:
- ✅ Emergency contact name
- ✅ Relationship (spouse, parent, sibling, friend)
- ✅ Emergency contact phone number

> **Important:** You must have a valid SIA license to work security shifts. Expired or invalid licenses will not be approved.

> **Note:** All information provided is kept strictly confidential and secure in compliance with GDPR regulations.

### Expected Timeline

- **Registration**: 2-3 minutes
- **Profile Completion**: 5-8 minutes
- **Approval Wait Time**: Typically 24-48 hours (varies by company)

**Total Active Time**: 5-10 minutes

## Registration Process

### Step 1: Access the Registration Page

1. Open the registration link provided by your employer
   - If you don't have a link, go to [yoursystem.com/register](yoursystem.com/register)

2. You'll see the registration form

### Step 2: Create Your Account

Fill in the following required information:

#### Username
- Choose a unique username for logging in
- Must be at least 4 characters
- Can contain letters, numbers, underscores, and hyphens
- Example: `john_smith` or `jsmith123`

> **Tip:** Choose something memorable but professional. You'll use this username every time you log in.

#### Email Address
- Enter your personal email address
- Must be valid – you'll receive important notifications here
- Example: `john.smith@email.com`
- Each email can only be used once in the system

> **Important:** Use an email address you check regularly. Shift notifications and important updates will be sent here.

#### Password
- Must meet security requirements:
  - ✅ Minimum 8 characters (12+ recommended)
  - ✅ At least one uppercase letter
  - ✅ At least one lowercase letter
  - ✅ At least one number
  - ✅ At least one special character (!, @, #, $, etc.)

> **Security Tip:** Use a strong, unique password. Consider using a password manager to generate and store complex passwords.

#### Full Name
- Enter your first name and last name
- This should match your SIA license exactly
- Example: `John David Smith`

#### Confirm Password
- Re-enter your password exactly as typed above
- This confirms you haven't made a typo

### Step 3: Submit Registration

1. Review all information for accuracy

2. Check the boxes:
   - ☑ **I agree to the Terms of Service**
   - ☑ **I agree to the Privacy Policy**

3. Click **"Sign Up"** or **"Register"**

### Step 4: Registration Confirmation

Upon successful registration:

- ✅ You'll see a confirmation message
- ✅ You'll be automatically logged in
- ✅ You'll be redirected to the dashboard

> **Note:** Unlike company administrators, you won't see a 5-step onboarding wizard. Staff members go directly to profile completion.

### Registration Troubleshooting

**Problem**: "Username already exists"
- **Solution**: Try a different username. Add numbers or underscores (e.g., `jsmith` → `jsmith123`)

**Problem**: "Email already registered"
- **Solution**: This email is already in use. Try another email address or use "Forgot Password" if you created an account previously.

**Problem**: "Password doesn't meet requirements"
- **Solution**: Ensure your password has uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Try: `MyPassword123!`

**Problem**: "Registration failed" error
- **Solution**: Check your internet connection. Ensure all fields are filled correctly. Try refreshing the page and re-submitting.

## Completing Your Profile

### Accessing the Profile Page

1. Click on your name in the top right corner
2. Select **"My Profile"** from the dropdown

   OR

1. Click the **"Complete Profile"** notification banner

### Profile Sections

Your profile is divided into several sections. Complete all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).

#### Section 1: Personal Information

**Basic Details**:

1. **First Name*** (Required)
   - Your legal first name
   - Must match SIA license

2. **Last Name*** (Required)
   - Your legal surname
   - Must match SIA license

3. **Email Address*** (Pre-filled)
   - Your registered email
   - Can be updated if needed

4. **Phone Number*** (Required)
   - Mobile number for shift notifications
   - Include country code: `+44 7700 123456`

5. **Date of Birth*** (Required)
   - Used for age verification and compliance
   - Format: DD/MM/YYYY
   - Example: `15/05/1990`

6. **National Insurance Number*** (Required)
   - For payroll and tax purposes
   - Format: AB 12 34 56 C
   - Example: `QQ 12 34 56 C`

> **Important:** National Insurance Number is required by law for employment and payroll. It's kept securely encrypted.

**Address**:

7. **Street Address*** (Required)
   - Your current home address
   - Example: `123 High Street, Flat 4B`

8. **City*** (Required)
   - Example: `London`

9. **Postal Code*** (Required)
   - UK format: `SW1A 1AA`

10. **Country*** (Required)
    - Usually pre-selected based on company location

> **Note:** Address information is used for tax purposes and emergency contact. It must be accurate and current.

#### Section 2: SIA License Information

This is the most critical section. You cannot work shifts without a valid SIA license.

**License Details**:

1. **SIA License Type*** (Required)
   - Select from dropdown:
     - Door Supervisor
     - Security Guard
     - CCTV Operator (Public Space Surveillance)
     - Close Protection
     - Cash and Valuables in Transit
     - Key Holding
     - Vehicle Immobiliser

> **Important:** Select the exact license type shown on your SIA badge. If you hold multiple licenses, you can add additional licenses after initial profile approval.

2. **SIA License Number*** (Required)
   - 16-character alphanumeric code
   - Found on the front of your SIA badge
   - Format: 4 blocks of 4 characters
   - Example: `1234 5678 9012 3456`

> **Tip:** Enter the number carefully. The system may validate it against SIA databases.

3. **License Issue Date*** (Required)
   - The date your license was issued
   - Found on your SIA badge
   - Format: DD/MM/YYYY

4. **License Expiry Date*** (Required)
   - The date your license expires
   - Found on your SIA badge
   - Format: DD/MM/YYYY
   - **Must be in the future** – expired licenses will be rejected

> **Warning:** The system will alert you 60 days and 30 days before license expiry. Ensure you renew in time to avoid losing shift assignments.

**License Document Upload**:

5. **Upload SIA License Document*** (Required)
   - Click **"Choose File"** or **"Upload"**
   - Select a clear photo or scan of your SIA license
   - **Accepted formats**: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG
   - **Maximum file size**: 5MB
   - **Requirements**:
     - All text must be clearly readable
     - All four corners of the license visible
     - No glare or shadows obscuring information
     - Color photo preferred

> **Tip:** Take a photo with good lighting against a dark background. Ensure license number and expiry date are clearly visible.

**Verification Notes**:

6. **Additional License Information** (Optional)
   - Any additional notes about your license
   - Example: "Renewal application submitted" if near expiry

#### Section 3: Qualifications & Certifications (Optional but Recommended)

Add any additional security qualifications:

**Examples**:
- First Aid Certificate
- Conflict Management Training
- Fire Safety Training
- Counter-Terrorism Awareness
- Customer Service Training
- SIA Top-Up Training

For each qualification:
1. Click **"Add Qualification"**
2. Enter qualification name
3. Enter issuing body
4. Enter issue date and expiry (if applicable)
5. Upload certificate (optional)

> **Tip:** Additional qualifications may make you eligible for premium shifts with higher pay rates.

## Bank Details for Payments

Accurate bank details ensure you receive payment on time.

### Bank Account Information

1. **Account Holder Name*** (Required)
   - Must match the name on your bank account
   - Example: `John David Smith`

> **Important:** Name must match exactly. Mismatched names may cause payment delays or rejections.

2. **Bank Name** (Required)
   - Name of your bank
   - Example: `Barclays Bank`, `HSBC`, `Lloyds Bank`

3. **Account Number*** (Required)
   - Usually 8 digits
   - Example: `12345678`

4. **Sort Code*** (Required)
   - 6 digits, shown as 3 pairs
   - Format: `12-34-56`
   - Example: `40-47-84`

> **Security Note:** Your bank details are encrypted and stored securely. Only authorized payroll administrators can access them.

### Verifying Your Bank Details

Before saving:
1. Double-check all numbers for accuracy
2. Compare against your bank card or statement
3. Verify account holder name matches exactly

> **Warning:** Incorrect bank details will delay your first payment. Take time to verify accuracy.

### Alternative Payment Methods

If you don't have a UK bank account:
- Contact your manager about alternative payment arrangements
- Some companies support international bank transfers or payment services

## The Approval Process

### What Happens After You Submit

1. **Profile Submitted**
   - You'll see a confirmation: "Profile submitted for review"
   - Status changes to **"Pending Approval"**

2. **Manager Review**
   - A manager or admin reviews your profile
   - They verify:
     - All required information is complete
     - SIA license is valid and not expired
     - SIA license document is clear and readable
     - Bank details appear correct
     - Emergency contact information is provided

3. **SIA License Verification**
   - Manager may verify license against SIA database
   - Checks for:
     - Valid license number
     - Correct license type
     - Current expiry date
     - No suspensions or revocations

4. **Decision**
   - **Approved**: You can now be assigned to shifts
   - **Corrections Requested**: You'll receive specific feedback on what needs fixing

### Timeline

**Typical Approval Time**: 24-48 hours

**Factors Affecting Approval Time**:
- Time of submission (weekends may take longer)
- Company workload
- How complete your profile is
- Whether additional verification is needed

> **Note:** You'll receive an email notification when your profile status changes.

### What You Can Do While Waiting

During the approval period:

✅ **You CAN**:
- Access the dashboard
- View company information
- Download the mobile app
- Explore the system interface
- Read shift policies and procedures
- Update your profile if you notice errors

❌ **You CANNOT**:
- Be assigned to shifts
- Claim open shifts
- Check in/out of venues
- Submit timesheets (no shifts yet)
- Receive payments (no worked shifts yet)

### If Corrections Are Requested

If your profile needs changes:

1. You'll receive an email with specific issues:
   - Example: "SIA license image is too blurry. Please upload a clearer photo."
   - Example: "License expiry date has passed. Please provide renewed license."
   - Example: "National Insurance Number format is invalid. Please check and correct."

2. **Fix the Issues**:
   - Go to **Profile** → **Edit**
   - Make required corrections
   - Re-upload documents if needed

3. **Resubmit**:
   - Click **"Save"** or **"Submit for Review"**
   - Profile returns to pending approval
   - Manager reviews again

> **Important:** Respond promptly to correction requests. Delayed responses mean delayed approval and shift assignments.

### Approval Notification

When approved, you'll receive:

- ✅ Email notification: "Your profile has been approved!"
- ✅ Dashboard notification
- ✅ Access to shift scheduling features
- ✅ Ability to claim open shifts

**Next Steps After Approval**:
1. Download the mobile app (if not already done)
2. Set up mobile notifications
3. Review your availability settings
4. Browse available shifts
5. Wait for your first shift assignment

## What Happens After Approval

### Your Dashboard

Once approved, your dashboard shows:

**Overview Section**:
- **Upcoming Shifts**: Next 7 days of assigned shifts
- **Available Shifts**: Open shifts you can claim
- **Recent Activity**: Latest updates and notifications
- **Quick Stats**: Total shifts this month, hours worked, earnings to date

**Notifications**:
- New shift assignments
- Shift changes or cancellations
- Payment notifications
- Messages from managers
- License expiry reminders

### Managing Your Shifts

#### Assigned Shifts

**View Shifts**:
1. Navigate to **My Shifts**
2. See all assigned shifts in calendar or list view
3. Click on any shift for details:
   - Venue name and address
   - Date and time (start/end)
   - Shift type (security guard, door supervisor, etc.)
   - Pay rate
   - Special requirements
   - Manager contact

**Accept or Decline**:
- New shift assignments may require confirmation
- Click **"Accept"** to confirm availability
- Click **"Decline"** if you cannot work (provide reason)

> **Important:** Frequently declining shifts without valid reasons may affect future assignments.

#### Claiming Open Shifts

**How It Works**:
1. Navigate to **Available Shifts**
2. Browse shifts that match your SIA license type
3. Filter by:
   - Date range
   - Venue location
   - Shift type
   - Pay rate

4. Click **"Claim Shift"** on any open shift
5. Manager receives your claim request
6. Wait for approval (usually same day)

> **Tip:** Claims are usually first-come, first-served. Check available shifts regularly for best opportunities.

#### Shift Exchanges

**Requesting an Exchange**:
If you can no longer work an assigned shift:

1. Go to **My Shifts** → Select the shift
2. Click **"Request Exchange"**
3. Select reason: Holiday, Emergency, Illness, Other
4. Choose exchange method:
   - **Swap with another staff member** (select colleague)
   - **Release to open shifts** (anyone can claim)
5. Submit request
6. Wait for manager approval

> **Note:** Exchange requests must be submitted at least 24 hours before shift start (check company policy for exact timeframe).

### Checking In and Out

**Mobile Check-In Required**:
- All shift check-ins must be done via mobile app
- GPS location verification ensures you're at the venue
- Photo verification may be required

**Check-In Process**:
1. Open mobile app 15 minutes before shift start
2. Navigate to **Current Shift**
3. Click **"Check In"**
4. Allow location permissions
5. System verifies you're within venue GPS boundary
6. Take selfie if requested (facial recognition)
7. Provide digital signature
8. Check-in confirmed

**Check-Out Process**:
1. At shift end, open mobile app
2. Navigate to **Current Shift**
3. Click **"Check Out"**
4. Verify location (must still be at venue)
5. Provide digital signature
6. Add shift notes if needed:
   - Incident reports
   - Unusual occurrences
   - Maintenance issues
7. Check-out confirmed

> **Important:** Failure to check in/out properly may result in timesheet delays or payment issues.

> **Warning:** Attempting to check in/out from outside the venue location will fail. GPS spoofing is detected and may result in disciplinary action.

### Timesheets

**Automatic Generation**:
- Timesheets are automatically created from your check-in/out times
- Review timesheets weekly: **Timesheets** → **View Timesheets**

**What to Check**:
- ✅ Shift dates and times are correct
- ✅ Total hours match your worked time
- ✅ Break deductions are accurate
- ✅ Pay rate is correct
- ✅ No missing shifts

**Corrections**:
If you spot errors:
1. Click **"Dispute Timesheet"**
2. Select the specific entry with the error
3. Explain the issue:
   - Example: "Worked until 11pm, not 10pm as shown"
   - Example: "Missed lunch break, should be paid for full time"
4. Submit dispute
5. Manager reviews and corrects if valid

> **Tip:** Review timesheets every week to catch errors early. Corrections are easier to make before payroll processing.

### Invoices & Payments

**Payment Schedule**:
- Check with your company for payment frequency
- Common schedules: Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly

**Viewing Invoices**:
1. Navigate to **Invoices**
2. See list of all invoices (pay stubs)
3. Click on any invoice for details:
   - Pay period
   - Shifts worked (dates, hours, pay rate)
   - Gross pay
   - Deductions (tax, NI)
   - Net pay (take-home)

**Payment Notifications**:
- Email notification when payment is processed
- Funds typically arrive 1-3 business days after processing
- Payments go to the bank account you provided in your profile

> **Important:** If payment doesn't arrive within expected timeframe, check your bank details in your profile are correct. Contact your manager if issues persist.

### Messages & Communication

**Receiving Messages**:
- Managers can send you direct messages
- Receive messages about:
  - Shift assignments
  - Schedule changes
  - Policy updates
  - Performance feedback
  - General announcements

**Sending Messages**:
1. Navigate to **Messages**
2. Click **"New Message"**
3. Select recipient (your manager or admin)
4. Type your message
5. Send

**Message Types**:
- **Urgent**: Shift issues, emergencies
- **Administrative**: Bank details, profile changes

> **Tip:** For urgent matters (shift emergencies), call your manager directly. Messages may not be read immediately.

### Using GPS Check-In

**How GPS Verification Works**:
1. Venue has a defined GPS boundary (geofence)
2. You must be physically within this boundary to check in
3. System uses your phone's location to verify

**Best Practices**:
- Enable location services before arriving at venue
- Ensure GPS signal is strong (avoid checking in from indoor basement areas)
- Check in within the first 15 minutes of shift start
- Don't attempt to check in while en route – wait until you arrive

**Troubleshooting GPS Issues**:

**Problem**: "Not at venue location" error
- Walk closer to the main venue entrance
- Ensure location services are enabled
- Check GPS signal strength
- Wait 30 seconds for GPS to update

**Problem**: GPS location not updating
- Toggle airplane mode on and off
- Restart location services
- Restart the app
- Ensure you have internet connection (GPS alone isn't enough)

### Offline Mode

**What Works Offline**:
- ✅ View previously loaded shifts

- ✅ View profile information

**What Doesn't Work Offline**:
- ❌ Check in/out (requires internet)

- ❌ Send messages
- ❌ Update profile

> **Important:** Check-in and check-out require internet connection. Ensure you have mobile data or Wi-Fi access at venues.

### Mobile App Support

- Reinstall app if problems persist

## Troubleshooting

### Profile Issues

**Problem**: "SIA license already exists in system"
**Solution**:
- This license may be registered under a different account
- Contact your manager to deactivate the old account
- Ensure you're not accidentally creating a duplicate account

**Problem**: Can't upload SIA license document
**Solution**:
- Check file size (max 5MB)
- Check file format (PDF, JPG, PNG only)
- Try a different file format
- Reduce image resolution if too large
- Ensure stable internet connection

**Problem**: Profile stuck in "Pending" for over 3 days
**Solution**:
- Send a polite message to your manager requesting status update
- Check email spam folder for correction requests
- Verify all required fields are completed
- Contact support if no response after 5 business days

### Login & Access Issues

**Problem**: Forgot password
**Solution**:
1. Click **"Forgot Password"** on login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check email for password reset link (check spam folder)
4. Click link and create new password
5. Login with new password

**Problem**: Account locked after failed login attempts
**Solution**:
- Wait 15 minutes for automatic unlock
- Use **"Forgot Password"** to reset
- Contact manager if urgent

**Problem**: "Profile not approved" message when trying to claim shifts
**Solution**:
- Profile is still pending manager approval
- Check email for correction requests
- Wait for approval notification
- Contact manager if approval is urgent

### Shift & Scheduling Issues

**Problem**: Not seeing any available shifts
**Solution**:
- Verify your profile is approved
- Check your availability settings (may be too restrictive)
- Expand travel radius in preferences
- Check if SIA license type matches available shifts
- Ask manager about upcoming shift availability

**Problem**: Shift disappeared from my schedule
**Solution**:
- Check for cancellation notification (email/app)
- Contact manager to confirm cancellation reason
- Check if shift was reassigned due to exchange request

**Problem**: Can't check in to shift
**Solution**:
- Ensure you're physically at the venue
- Enable location services on phone
- Check GPS signal strength
- Verify shift start time is within 15-minute window
- Try moving to different location at venue (better GPS signal)
- Restart app and try again
- Contact manager if issue persists (may need manual check-in)

### Payment & Timesheet Issues

**Problem**: Payment didn't arrive on expected date
**Solution**:
1. Verify payment processing date (check invoice)
2. Check bank details in profile are correct
3. Allow 1-3 business days for bank transfer
4. Contact manager or payroll department after 5 business days

**Problem**: Timesheet shows incorrect hours
**Solution**:
1. Click **"Dispute Timesheet"**
2. Provide correct times with explanation
3. Include evidence if available (photos, messages)
4. Manager will review and correct

**Problem**: Missing shift on timesheet
**Solution**:
- Verify you checked in and out properly
- Check if shift is on different pay period
- Contact manager with shift details (date, venue, times)

### Mobile App Issues

**Problem**: App crashes on launch
**Solution**:
- Update app to latest version
- Restart phone
- Clear app cache and data
- Reinstall app
- Contact support with device model and OS version

**Problem**: Push notifications not working
**Solution**:
- Check notification permissions in phone settings
- Enable notifications in app settings
- Check Do Not Disturb mode isn't active
- Reinstall app if problem persists

**Problem**: GPS location always shows as "Not at venue"

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**Q: How long does profile approval take?**
A: Typically 24-48 hours. Approval time depends on manager workload and completeness of your profile. Weekend submissions may take until Monday.

**Q: Can I start working before my profile is approved?**
A: No. You must be approved before being assigned to any shifts. This ensures compliance with SIA licensing requirements.

**Q: What if I don't have a SIA license yet?**
A: You cannot work security shifts without a valid SIA license. Complete your SIA training and obtain your license before registering. Contact your manager about non-security roles if available.

**Q: Can I work for multiple security companies using this system?**
A: Yes, if you're registered with multiple companies that use the system. Each company requires separate profile approval.

**Q: Do I need a smartphone to work?**
A: Yes, a smartphone with GPS is required for shift check-in/out. The system supports iOS (iPhone 8 or newer) and Android (version 8.0 or newer).

### SIA License & Compliance

**Q: My SIA license is expiring soon. What should I do?**
A: Renew your license with the SIA immediately. Update your profile with new license details as soon as you receive the renewed license. The system sends reminders 60 and 30 days before expiry.

**Q: Can I work with an expired SIA license?**
A: No. Working with an expired license is illegal and you will be removed from all shifts immediately. Renew your license before expiry.

**Q: I have multiple SIA license types. Do I need to enter all of them?**
A: Enter your primary license during registration. After approval, you can add additional licenses in your profile to qualify for more shift types.

**Q: What if my SIA license is suspended?**
A: Notify your manager immediately. You will be removed from all shifts until suspension is lifted. Update your profile with a new license once suspension ends.

### Shifts & Scheduling

**Q: How do I know when I'm assigned to a shift?**
A: You'll receive notifications via email, SMS (if enabled), and push notification (mobile app). Check **My Shifts** regularly.

**Q: Can I decline a shift I've been assigned to?**
A: Yes, but provide a valid reason (illness, emergency, prior commitment). Frequent declines without good reason may affect future assignments.

**Q: How do open shift claims work?**
A: Browse **Available Shifts**, click **"Claim Shift"** on any that interest you. Manager reviews claims (usually first-come, first-served) and approves. You'll receive notification of approval or denial.

**Q: What's the minimum notice period for shift assignments?**
A: Check company policy. Common standards are 24-48 hours, but emergency shifts may have shorter notice.

**Q: Can I request specific shifts or venues?**
A: Set preferences in your profile (preferred venues, available days). While not guaranteed, managers try to accommodate preferences when possible.

**Q: What if I'm running late to a shift?**
A: Contact your manager or venue supervisor immediately. Explain reason and estimated arrival time. Arriving late without notice may result in shift cancellation.

**Q: Can I swap shifts with other staff?**
A: Yes. Use the **Shift Exchange** feature. Select the shift, choose a colleague to swap with, submit request. Manager must approve the exchange.

### Check-In/Out & Timesheets

**Q: How early can I check in to a shift?**
A: Usually 15 minutes before scheduled start time. Check company policy for exact window.

**Q: What if I forget to check out?**
A: Contact your manager immediately. They can manually check you out. Frequent forgotten check-outs may trigger reminders or disciplinary action.

**Q: Can I check in/out from home?**
A: No. GPS verification ensures you're physically at the venue. Attempting to fake location is a serious violation.

**Q: What if GPS isn't working at the venue?**
A: Try moving to different location at venue for better signal. If still failing, contact manager for manual check-in. Take a photo as proof you're on-site.

**Q: How are breaks handled?**
A: Breaks are typically unpaid and deducted from total hours. Check company policy for break requirements (usually 20 min for 6+ hour shifts in UK).

**Q: When are timesheets submitted?**
A: Timesheets are automatically generated from check-in/out data. Review and approve weekly. Manager final-approves before payroll processing.

**Q: How do I dispute incorrect timesheet hours?**
A: Click **"Dispute Timesheet"** on the entry, explain the error, provide correct times. Manager reviews and corrects if valid.

### Payments

**Q: When will I receive my first payment?**
A: After completing shifts, timesheets being approved, and reaching your company's pay period end. First payment may take 2-4 weeks depending on when you start and payment schedule.

**Q: How often am I paid?**
A: Check with your company. Common schedules: weekly, bi-weekly (every 2 weeks), or monthly.

**Q: What if my bank details change?**
A: Update immediately in **Profile** → **Bank Details**. Changes take effect for next payment cycle. Notify manager of the change.

**Q: How is tax handled?**
A: Tax and National Insurance are automatically deducted from your pay. You'll receive payslips showing gross pay, deductions, and net pay. Keep these for tax records.

**Q: Can I view payslips?**
A: Yes. Navigate to **Invoices** to see all payslips. Download PDF copies for your records.

**Q: What if I don't receive payment on time?**
A: First, check your bank details are correct in profile. Allow 1-3 business days for bank transfer. If still missing after 5 days, contact payroll or your manager.
