## Before You Begin

### What You'll Need

To complete the onboarding process smoothly, please have the following information ready:

**Company Information**:
- ✅ Company legal name and registration number
- ✅ Business address (street, city, postal code, country)
- ✅ Industry type and company size
- ✅ Primary contact details (name, email, phone)
- ✅ Billing email address

**Compliance Information**:
- ✅ Primary operating region/jurisdiction
- ✅ Any additional regions where you operate
- ✅ Required compliance certifications (if applicable)
- ✅ Data protection requirements

**Operational Details**:
- ✅ Current staff count or expected initial size
- ✅ Growth projections (6 months, 1 year, 2 years)
- ✅ Peak operational capacity needs

## Registration & First Login

### Creating Your Account

1. Navigate to the registration page
2. Fill in the required information:
   - Username (unique identifier for login)
   - Email address (must be valid)
   - Password (must meet security requirements)
   - Full name

3. Click **"Sign Up"** to create your account

> **Important:** Use a strong password that includes uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

### First Login Experience

After successful registration:

1. You'll be automatically logged in
2. The system detects that you haven't completed onboarding
3. You'll be redirected to the onboarding wizard automatically

> **Note:** Your account is initially created with "staff" privileges. Once you complete the onboarding wizard and create your company, you'll automatically become the company owner with full administrative access.

## Step 2: Regional Compliance Setup

Configure your company's compliance requirements based on your operating regions. This ensures your system adheres to local labor laws, security regulations, and data protection requirements.

### Screenshot Placeholder
```
[Screenshot: Step 2 - Regional Compliance page showing region selection and compliance profile]
```

### What to Complete

#### Section A: Primary Region Selection

1. **Select Your Primary Operating Region** (Required)
   - Choose the main jurisdiction where your company operates
   - This determines default compliance settings
   - Example: "United Kingdom - England"

> **Important:** The primary region affects working hour regulations, overtime rules, and statutory requirements. Choose carefully.

#### Section B: Additional Operating Regions

If your company operates across multiple regions:

1. **Select Additional Regions** (Optional)
   - Multi-select dropdown for additional jurisdictions
   - Example: "Scotland", "Wales", "Northern Ireland"

2. **Regional Considerations**
   - Each selected region may have specific compliance requirements
   - The system will highlight region-specific regulations

> **Tip:** Only select regions where you actively employ staff. You can add more regions later as your business expands.

#### Section C: Compliance Profile

The system automatically populates recommended settings based on your selected regions. Review and adjust as needed:

**Working Hours & Overtime**:

1. **Working Hours Regulation**
   - Standard working hours per week
   - Example: "48 hours maximum per week (Working Time Directive)"
   - Options: 35h, 40h, 45h, 48h, Custom

2. **Overtime Rules**
   - How overtime is calculated and paid
   - Options:
     - Time and a half after 40 hours
     - Double time after 48 hours
     - Flat rate overtime
     - Custom rules

3. **Break Requirements**
   - Mandatory rest periods
   - Example: "20 minutes for every 6 hours worked"
   - Default based on region (UK: 20 min/6h, 11h daily rest, 24h weekly rest)

**Leave & Public Holidays**:

4. **Public Holidays**
   - Select applicable public holidays for your regions
   - Automatically populated based on primary region
   - Can be customized

5. **Annual Leave Entitlement**
   - Statutory minimum leave days
   - Example: "28 days (including public holidays)" for UK
   - Can be increased for company policy

6. **Leave Accrual Method**
   - How leave is earned
   - Options: Monthly accrual, Annual allocation, Pro-rata

#### Section D: Special Requirements

Select any additional compliance requirements for your operations:

1. **High Security Clearance**
   - Required for sensitive sites (government, defense, etc.)

2. **Biometric Verification**
   - Fingerprint or facial recognition for shift verification

3. **Enhanced Background Checks**
   - DBS checks, criminal record checks

4. **Drug & Alcohol Testing**
   - Random or scheduled testing requirements

5. **Night Work Restrictions**
   - Special regulations for overnight shifts
   - Health assessments for night workers

6. **Youth Employment Restrictions**
   - If employing workers under 18 years old
   - Additional break requirements and hour restrictions

> **Warning:** Selecting special requirements will add mandatory checks to your staff onboarding and shift management processes. Ensure you have the infrastructure to support these requirements.

#### Section E: Data Protection Level

Select the appropriate data protection compliance level:

1. **Basic**
   - Standard password protection and secure storage
   - Suitable for small operations with minimal data

2. **GDPR Compliant** (Recommended for UK/EU)
   - Full GDPR compliance
   - Data processing agreements
   - Right to erasure, data portability
   - Required for UK and EU operations

3. **Enhanced**
   - GDPR + additional encryption
   - Detailed audit trails
   - Advanced access controls

4. **Enterprise**
   - Maximum security level
   - End-to-end encryption
   - Dedicated compliance officer
   - Regular security audits

> **Important:** For UK and EU operations, you must select at least "GDPR Compliant" level. Non-compliance can result in significant fines.

### What Happens When You Click "Next"

1. Your regional compliance settings are validated
2. The system configures compliance rules for your company
3. Staff operational templates are prepared based on your selections
4. Step 2 is marked as complete (40% progress)
5. You're redirected to Step 3: Staff Operations Configuration

### Best Practices

> **Tip:** When in doubt, select stricter compliance options. You can always relax requirements later, but tightening them retrospectively can be challenging.

> **Tip:** Review your local labor laws before completing this step. The system provides recommendations but cannot replace legal advice.

### Troubleshooting Step 2

**Problem**: "Region not found" or dropdown won't load
- **Solution**: Check your internet connection. If the problem persists, refresh the page (your progress is saved).

**Problem**: Unsure about overtime rules
- **Solution**: Check your employment contracts or consult with your HR advisor. The system allows custom rules if standard options don't match your needs.

**Problem**: Don't know which data protection level to choose
- **Solution**: If operating in the UK/EU, select "GDPR Compliant" at minimum. Contact support for guidance on Enhanced or Enterprise levels.

## Step 3: Staff Operations Configuration

Configure your company's operational capacity and staff management settings. This step helps the system understand your staffing needs and growth plans.

### Screenshot Placeholder
```
[Screenshot: Step 3 - Staff Operations page showing staff size, growth projections, and capacity sliders]
```

### What to Complete

#### Section A: Current Staff Size

1. **How many security staff do you currently employ?** (Required)
   - Select from dropdown:
     - 1-10 staff
     - 11-50 staff
     - 51-200 staff
     - 200+ staff

> **Note:** This helps determine your subscription tier and system limits. Select your current size, not projected future size.

#### Section B: Growth Projections

Help us understand your expansion plans:

1. **Expected Staff in 6 Months**
   - Use the +/- controls or type a number
   - Default is current staff + 20%

2. **Expected Staff in 1 Year**
   - Future planning for system capacity
   - Default is current staff + 50%

3. **Expected Staff in 2 Years**
   - Long-term growth projection
   - Default is current staff + 100%

> **Tip:** These projections are used for capacity planning and upgrade recommendations. You won't be charged based on projections, only actual usage.

#### Section C: Operational Capacity

Configure your maximum operational parameters using the sliders:

**1. Maximum Concurrent Shifts** (Range: 1-100)
- How many shifts can run simultaneously at peak times?
- Example: If you have 5 venues with 4 guards each = 20 concurrent shifts
- **Default**: Based on current staff size
- **Recommended**: Current staff × 1.5

**2. Peak Hours Capacity** (Range: 1-200)
- Maximum staff working during your busiest periods
- Consider weekend events, night shifts, special events
- Example: Friday/Saturday nights might require 80% of total staff
- **Default**: Based on current staff size
- **Recommended**: Current staff × 0.8

**3. Emergency Staffing Reserve** (Range: 1-50)
- How many staff can you deploy on short notice?
- Important for call-outs, sick cover, unexpected events
- Example: 10-15% of total staff
- **Default**: 10% of current staff
- **Recommended**: 10-20% of current staff

**4. Special Event Capacity** (Range: 1-500)
- Maximum staff for large events (concerts, festivals, conferences)
- Consider temporary staff, contractors, partnerships
- Example: Music festival requiring 200 security personnel
- **Default**: Current staff × 2
- **Recommended**: Plan for your largest anticipated event

> **Important:** These capacity settings create soft limits in the system. You'll receive warnings when approaching limits but can exceed them if necessary. They help with resource planning and scheduling optimization.

### Understanding Capacity Calculations

The system uses your inputs to calculate:

- **Staff Capacity Limit**: Based on your current size selection
  - 1-10 staff → Limit: 15
  - 11-50 staff → Limit: 75
  - 51-200 staff → Limit: 300
  - 200+ staff → Limit: 1000

- **Venue Capacity**: Based on operational needs
  - Calculated from concurrent shifts and peak capacity
  - Can be adjusted later in company settings

### What Happens When You Click "Next"

1. Operational capacity settings are validated
2. Your company's `staff_capacity` and `venue_capacity` limits are set
3. Shift scheduling templates are configured
4. Step 3 is marked as complete (60% progress)
5. You're redirected to Step 4: Integrations Setup

### Best Practices

> **Tip:** It's better to overestimate capacity needs initially. The system performs better with headroom than with constrained limits.

> **Tip:** Review your busiest historical periods (holiday seasons, summer events) when setting peak capacity.

> **Warning:** Setting very high capacity limits on smaller subscription tiers may trigger upgrade prompts. Choose realistic numbers based on actual needs.

### Troubleshooting Step 3

**Problem**: Slider won't move or number won't change
- **Solution**: Try clicking directly on the track or using keyboard arrow keys. Ensure JavaScript is enabled.

**Problem**: Unsure what numbers to enter
- **Solution**: Use the recommended defaults and adjust later. You can modify these settings anytime from the company settings page.

**Problem**: "Capacity exceeds subscription limits" warning
- **Solution**: Either reduce your capacity settings to match your current plan or consider upgrading to a higher tier during Step 5.

### Overview

Integrations allow you to:
- ✅ Sync employee data with workforce management systems
- ✅ Automate timesheet and roster imports
- ✅ Streamline invoicing and accounting
- ✅ Integrate payroll processing
- ✅ Connect communication platforms

> **Note:** You can skip this step entirely and configure integrations later from the company settings. Click **"Skip"** at the bottom to proceed to Step 5.

### Available Integrations

#### Integration 1: Deputy Workforce Management

**What is Deputy?**
Deputy is a workforce management platform for scheduling, timesheets, and employee management.

**Benefits of Integration**:
- Automatic employee synchronization
- Timesheet import (eliminates double-entry)
- Roster/schedule synchronization
- Location data sync

**What You Need**:
1. **Deputy Account**
   - Active Deputy subscription
   - Admin access to your Deputy account

2. **API Credentials**:
   - **API Key**: Found in Deputy Settings → Integrations → API Access
   - **Subdomain**: Your Deputy subdomain (e.g., "yourcompany" from yourcompany.deputy.com)

**Configuration Steps**:

1. **Enable Deputy Integration**
   - Toggle the switch to **ON**

2. **Enter API Key** (Required)
   - Copy from Deputy: Settings → Integrations → API Access → Generate API Key
   - Paste into the API Key field

3. **Enter Subdomain** (Required)
   - Your company's Deputy subdomain
   - Example: If your Deputy URL is "securesolutions.deputy.com", enter "securesolutions"

4. **Select Sync Frequency**
   - How often data syncs between systems
   - Options: Real-time, Every 15 minutes, Hourly, Daily
   - **Recommended**: Every 15 minutes (balances freshness and API usage)

5. **Choose What to Sync**:
   - ☑ **Sync Employees**: Import employee records from Deputy
   - ☑ **Sync Timesheets**: Import worked hours automatically
   - ☑ **Sync Rosters**: Import scheduled shifts
   - ☑ **Sync Locations**: Import venue/location data

6. **Test Connection**
   - Click **"Test Connection"** button
   - Wait for success confirmation
   - If it fails, double-check credentials

> **Important:** The initial sync may take several minutes depending on how many employees and timesheets you have in Deputy.

> **Warning:** Ensure the API key has appropriate permissions in Deputy. Limited keys may cause sync failures.

#### Integration 2: Accounting Software

Connect your accounting platform to automate invoicing and financial reporting.

**Supported Providers**:
- Xero
- QuickBooks Online
- Sage Business Cloud
- Zoho Books

**Benefits**:
- Automatic invoice creation
- Expense tracking synchronization
- Payroll cost integration
- Tax reporting automation

**Configuration Steps**:

1. **Select Accounting Provider** (Required)
   - Choose from the dropdown: Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, Zoho

2. **Enter Client ID** (Required)
   - OAuth Client ID from your accounting software
   - Where to find:
     - **Xero**: Developer Portal → My Apps → OAuth Credentials
     - **QuickBooks**: Intuit Developer Portal → App Dashboard
     - **Sage**: Developer Portal → Applications
     - **Zoho**: API Console → Client Secret

3. **Enter Client Secret** (Required)
   - OAuth Client Secret (keep this confidential)
   - Found in the same location as Client ID

4. **Choose What to Sync**:
   - ☑ **Sync Invoices**: Create accounting invoices automatically
   - ☑ **Sync Expenses**: Import expense claims
   - ☑ **Sync Payroll**: Export payroll costs
   - ☑ **Sync Taxes**: Tax calculation and reporting

5. **Authorize Connection**
   - Click **"Authorize"** to connect
   - You'll be redirected to your accounting provider
   - Sign in and grant permissions
   - Return to the onboarding wizard

> **Tip:** We recommend starting with invoice sync only, then enabling other features once you've confirmed the integration works correctly.

#### Integration 3: Payroll System

Integrate payroll processing to automate staff payments.

**What You Need**:
- Payroll provider account (compatible with your accounting software)
- Provider API credentials or integration key

**Configuration Steps**:

1. **Select Payroll Provider**
   - Choose your payroll software
   - Options depend on your region and accounting integration

2. **Enter Provider API Key**
   - Found in your payroll software's integration or API settings

3. **Set Pay Frequency**
   - Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly
   - Must match your actual payroll schedule

4. **Test Connection**
   - Verify credentials before proceeding

> **Important:** Payroll integration requires your accounting integration to be configured first for most providers.

#### Integration 4: Communication Platform (Advanced)

Connect team communication tools for automated notifications.

**Supported Platforms**:
- Slack
- Microsoft Teams
- WhatsApp Business API

**Benefits**:
- Automatic shift notifications
- Staff alerts for new schedules
- Emergency broadcast messages
- Admin notifications for approvals

**Setup**:
These integrations require additional configuration and webhook setup. We recommend configuring these after completing onboarding from the company settings page.

### Testing Your Integrations

After configuring each integration:

1. Click the **"Test Connection"** button
2. Wait for the system to verify credentials
3. Look for success confirmation (green checkmark)
4. If test fails, review error message and correct credentials

> **Warning:** Do not proceed if connection tests fail. Invalid credentials may cause sync issues later.

### What Happens When You Click "Next" or "Skip"

**If you configured integrations**:
1. Integration records are created for each enabled service
2. Initial sync is triggered in the background
3. Health monitoring is activated
4. Step 4 is marked as complete (80% progress)

**If you skip this step**:
1. No integration records are created
2. Step 4 is marked as complete without configuration
3. You can add integrations later from company settings
4. 80% progress achieved

You're redirected to Step 5: Account Finalization

### Best Practices

> **Tip:** If you're unsure about integrations, skip this step and complete basic onboarding first. You can return to integrations once you're familiar with the core system.

> **Tip:** Test integrations during a low-activity period. The initial sync can temporarily slow down your accounting software if you have many records.

> **Warning:** Keep API keys and secrets confidential. Never share them via email or unsecured channels.

### Troubleshooting Step 4

**Problem**: "Invalid API key" error for Deputy
- **Solution**: Verify the API key is copied correctly with no extra spaces. Ensure the key hasn't expired. Generate a new key in Deputy if needed.

**Problem**: Accounting authorization fails
- **Solution**: Ensure pop-ups aren't blocked. Check that you're logged into your accounting software in another browser tab. Verify your account has admin permissions.

**Problem**: Connection test times out
- **Solution**: Check your internet connection. The third-party service may be temporarily unavailable. Try again in a few minutes.

**Problem**: Don't have integration credentials ready
- **Solution**: Click **"Skip"** to bypass this step. You can configure integrations later from Settings → Integrations.

### Common Issues

#### Can't Access Dashboard After Onboarding

**Symptoms**: Redirected back to onboarding or login page

**Solutions**:
1. Clear browser cache and cookies
2. Log out completely and log back in
3. Verify you received the "Onboarding Complete" confirmation

5. Try a different browser or incognito mode
6. Contact support if issue persists

#### Invited Admins Not Receiving Emails

**Solutions**:
1. Check spam/junk folders
2. Verify email addresses are correct (Settings → Team)
3. Resend invitation from team management page
4. Add noreply@yoursystem.com to safe senders
5. Check email server isn't blocking automated emails
6. Try alternative email address

#### Integration Sync Not Working

**Solutions**:
1. Navigate to Settings → Integrations
2. Check integration status (should show "Active")
3. Click "Test Connection" to verify credentials
4. Review "Last Sync" timestamp
5. Check error logs for specific issues
6. Re-enter API credentials if needed
7. Contact integration provider support for API issues

#### Staff Can't Register or Log In

**Solutions**:
1. Verify they're using the correct registration link
2. Check for typos in email addresses
3. Ensure they meet password requirements
4. Try password reset if login fails
5. Check if browser is blocking cookies
6. Clear browser cache
7. Verify company is active (Settings → Company)

#### Payment/Subscription Issues

**Solutions**:
1. Verify payment method is valid and not expired
2. Check for sufficient funds/credit limit
3. Contact your bank if payment declined
4. Try alternative payment method
5. Review invoice for any discrepancies
6. Contact billing@yoursystem.com for billing questions

#### Mobile App Not Syncing

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure staff have completed profile setup

4. Update app to latest version
5. Log out and log back in
6. Reinstall app if necessary
7. Check if company integration is enabled
