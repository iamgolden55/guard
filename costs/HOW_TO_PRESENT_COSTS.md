# How to Present the Cost Breakdown to Your Client

## Quick Strategy Guide

### 🎯 Your Goal
Get agreement on **£6,500 remaining** (£5,000 dev + £1,500 AWS setup) + **£150/month** infrastructure

### 🔑 Key Messages
1. **You're getting 3 apps, not 1**: Backend + Web + Mobile (scope increased 150%)
2. **It's 85% done**: Just needs finishing and enhancement phases
3. **ROI in 18 months**: Cheaper than SaaS long-term (saves £2-4k/year after)
4. **Professional infrastructure**: AWS is enterprise-grade, not cheap hosting

---

## 🗣️ Conversation Script

### Opening (Frame the Conversation)

**YOU**: "Thanks for meeting. I've prepared a transparent breakdown of where we are and what's remaining. The good news: the system is 85% functional and working well. The full picture requires a conversation about scope changes."

### Present the Facts

**YOU**: "When we started at £7k, the agreement covered a basic web application. During development, we expanded to include:
- A full mobile app (iOS + Android)
- Offline capabilities for field staff
- Advanced shift transfer system
- Push notifications
- Virtual ID cards with QR codes

Essentially, you're getting 3 complete applications instead of 1."

### Show the Numbers

**YOU**: "Here's where we are:
- **Completed**: £2,000 (already paid) ✅
- **85% Done**: £2,250 (leave management, shift transfers, notifications)
- **Remaining Features**: £2,750 (virtual ID, attendance, compliance)
- **AWS Deployment**: £1,500 (production setup, app stores)
- **Total Outstanding**: £6,500

The monthly AWS cost of £150 covers enterprise-grade infrastructure - automated backups, security, push notifications, global CDN."

### Handle the Price Difference

**CLIENT**: "But we agreed on £7,000 total..."

**YOU**: "Absolutely, and I take responsibility for not catching the scope expansion earlier. Here's what changed:

| Original Scope | What You're Getting | Impact |
|----------------|---------------------|---------|
| Web app only | Web + iOS + Android | +100% platforms |
| Basic features | Advanced features + offline | +150% functionality |
| Shared hosting | AWS enterprise infrastructure | Professional grade |

You're getting roughly **2.5x the original scope**. The revised cost of £8,500 is still fair given the deliverables."

### Offer Solutions

**YOU**: "I have two options for you:

**Option 1 - Complete Everything**: £6,500 total
- Get all features (virtual ID, attendance, compliance, analytics)
- Professional AWS deployment
- Timeline: 12-14 weeks
- Best long-term value

**Option 2 - MVP First**: £3,450 to launch
- Launch with what's 85% done (leave management, shift transfers)
- Simpler infrastructure (£80/month instead of £150)
- Add other features later as budget allows
- Timeline: 4 weeks to live

Which approach makes more sense for your budget?"

## 🛡️ Handling Objections

### "This is way over budget"

**Response**: "I understand. Let's look at Option 2 - MVP approach. You can launch with the core features for £3,450 and add the rest later. This gets you operational in 4 weeks and spreads the cost."

**Show This**:
- MVP: £3,450 now
- Phase 2 (later): £1,500 when ready
- Phase 3 (later): £875 when ready
- Total flexibility on timeline

---

### "Why is AWS so expensive?"

**Response**: "£150/month actually isn't expensive for what you get. Let me show you the breakdown:"

**Show This**:
| What You Need | DIY (Cheap) | AWS (Professional) |
|---------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Server | £5/month ❌ Can't run Django | £35/month ✅ Guaranteed uptime |
| Database | Manual setup ❌ No auto-backups | £45/month ✅ Automated backups |
| Notifications | Not included ❌ | £5/month ✅ Push to mobile |
| CDN | Not included ❌ | £12/month ✅ Fast globally |
| Monitoring | Manual ❌ | £8/month ✅ 24/7 alerts |

**Alternative**: "If you have an IT team, I can set you up for self-hosting and save the £150/month. But you'll need someone to manage backups, security updates, and monitoring."

---

### "We can't afford £6,500 right now"

**Response**: "I completely understand. Here's a payment plan:"

**Show This**:
- **Today**: £2,500 (gets Phase 1 & Mobile Phase 1 to 100%)
- **Month 2** (6 weeks): £2,000 (Virtual ID half done)
- **Month 3** (11 weeks): £2,000 (everything complete)

"This spreads it over 3 months while we keep making progress."

---

### "Can we get a discount?"

**Response**: "I'm already at cost on this - £25/hour is below market rate for specialized development. However, I can offer:"

**Discount Options**:
1. **Referral**: Refer another client, get 10% off (save £650)
2. **AWS Prepay**: Pay 12 months AWS upfront, get 2 months free (£1,500 vs £1,800)
3. **Maintenance Contract**: Sign 12-month maintenance, get £500 off development

---

### "We'll find someone cheaper to finish it"

**Response**: "That's your right, and I'll provide full handover. But consider:
- I know the entire codebase (340 hours of work)
- New developer needs 40+ hours just to understand it (£1,000+ in onboarding)
- They won't know the business logic or architecture decisions
- Risk of breaking existing functionality

It's usually more expensive to switch mid-project. But if you decide to, I'll ensure a smooth handover."

## 💡 Smart Tactics

### 1. Lead with Value, Not Cost

**DON'T SAY**: "It's going to cost £6,500 more..."
**DO SAY**: "You're getting a mobile app, offline capabilities, and professional infrastructure that will save you £2-4k/year compared to SaaS..."

---

### 2. Use the SaaS Comparison

Show them the alternative costs:

| Year | Your System | Deputy (SaaS) | Savings |
|------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Year 1 | £10,300 (build + run) | £4,800 | -£5,500 |
| Year 2 | £1,800 | £4,800 | +£3,000 |
| Year 3 | £1,800 | £4,800 | +£3,000 |
| **Total** | **£13,900** | **£14,400** | **+£500** |

**YOUR POINT**: "By Year 3, you've broken even. Every year after that, you save £3,000. Plus, you OWN the system - no vendor lock-in."

---

### 3. Show What's Already Working

Before talking money, **DEMO THE SYSTEM**:
1. Show the mobile app working
2. Show shift transfers in action
3. Show leave management
4. Show notifications

**YOUR POINT**: "This is already functional and providing value. We just need to finish and polish it."

---

### 4. Emphasize Sunk Cost

**SAY**: "You've already invested £2,000 and we're 85% done. Walking away now means:
- Losing the £2,000 investment
- No working system
- Starting from scratch elsewhere costs more

Finishing it for £6,500 gets you a complete, custom system worth £15-20k if you hired a traditional agency."

## 🚨 Red Flags to Watch For

If client says this → Here's what it means:

1. **"Let me think about it"** → They're not convinced of value
   - **Action**: Reinforce ROI and SaaS comparison

2. **"That's way too expensive"** → Budget shock
   - **Action**: Present MVP option immediately

3. **"We'll get back to you"** → They're shopping around
   - **Action**: Emphasize sunk cost and handover complexity

4. **"Can you do it for the original £7k?"** → Testing negotiation
   - **Action**: Explain scope changes, offer discounts if they commit today

5. **"We need to discuss internally"** → Financial approval needed
   - **Action**: Provide spreadsheet format for their finance team
